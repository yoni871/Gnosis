## Quotation
> the blessed Apostle Paul teaches the same thing, and sets forth the sacrament of unity, saying, There is one body and one spirit, one hope of your calling, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God?

- Location: On the Unity of the Church, §4
- Translation: Ernest Wallis
- Edition: Ante-Nicene Fathers, vol. 5 (1886)
- Language: English
- Reproducibility: public-domain


## Explanation

Cyprian cites Ephesians 4:4-6 as Paul teaching 'the same thing' as the Lord's words about Peter and the one Church: one body, one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God. The sevenfold oneness of the passage is, for Cyprian, the scriptural statement of the sacrament of unity.

## Relationship to doctrine

(C) Cyprian reads Ephesians 4:4-6 as the apostolic statement of the unity that the Church must hold. (A) The passage names one Spirit, one Lord, and one God together, but does not name Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as three persons, and does not state the Trinity. (D) Cyprian's use is ecclesiological (church unity grounded in divine unity), not a Nicene doctrinal formulation.

## Historical context

On the Unity of the Church, c. 251 (approximate), §4, written against the Novatian schism.
