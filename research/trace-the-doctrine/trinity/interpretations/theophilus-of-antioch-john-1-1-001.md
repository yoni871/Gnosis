## Quotation

## Explanation

In II.22 Theophilus explains how the uncontainable Father can be said to 'walk in Paradise': his Word, his power and wisdom, assuming the person of the Father, went to the garden. John is cited as one of the 'spirit-bearing men' who teach that the Word was with God, was God, and made all things.

## Relationship to the doctrine

This is a historical reading (row C): Theophilus reads John 1:1 as teaching that the Word was eternally in God and is God. Note his phrasing - 'at first God was alone, and the Word in Him' - which later theology would express differently; it is recorded as he wrote it (row D: not Nicene language).

## Historical context

To Autolycus, written c. 180 (approximate), is Theophilus's apology addressed to his pagan friend Autolycus. Book II walks through Genesis 1 day by day with allegorising commentary, and explains how the uncontainable Father can be said to walk in Paradise (ch. 22).

## Alternatives and disagreements

No second-century dissenting reading of John 1:1 was found in the sources checked.
