## Quotation

> And He is the Lord who received commission from the Lord who [remains] in the heavens, i.e., the Maker of all things, to inflict upon Sodom and Gomorrha the [judgments] which the Scripture describes in these terms: 'The Lord rained down upon Sodom and Gomorrha sulphur and fire from the Lord out of heaven.'

## Explanation

Justin argues that the Lord who rained fire on Sodom is the same God who appeared to Abraham, and that he received his commission from the Lord who remains in heaven. For Justin this shows Scripture itself speaking of one who is called Lord and God besides the Maker of all things, yet subject to him: 'another God and Lord subject to the Maker of all things.'

## Relationship to the doctrine

This is a historical reading (row C): Justin reads Genesis 19:24 as the Son receiving a commission from the Father to judge Sodom. The text itself (row A) says the LORD rained fire from the LORD; the identification of the two Lords as Son and Father is Justin's reading. His language of 'another God and Lord subject to the Maker' is recorded as he wrote it and must not be harmonised into later Nicene equality (row D).

## Historical context

Dialogue with Trypho, written c. 155-160 (range), is Justin's defence of Christianity in dialogue with the Jew Trypho, arguing from the Old Testament that Jesus is the Christ and is divine. The chapters cited here (56, 62) belong to the section in which Justin argues that the Scriptures speak of another God and Lord, distinct from the Maker yet subject to him.
