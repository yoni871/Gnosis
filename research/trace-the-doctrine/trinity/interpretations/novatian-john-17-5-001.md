## Quotation
> Repeating this same thing, He says: “Father, glorify me with that glory wherewith I was with Thee before the world was.”

- Location: Treatise Concerning the Trinity, ch. 13
- Translation: Robert Ernest Wallis
- Edition: Ante-Nicene Fathers, vol. 5 (1886)
- Language: English
- Reproducibility: public-domain


## Explanation

Novatian cites Christ's prayer in John 17:5 to show that the Word possessed glory with the Father before the world was, and that the flesh, ascending to where it had descended from, 'receives that glory which in being shown to have had before the foundation of the world, it is most manifestly proved to be God.' The pre-temporal glory is evidence, for Novatian, of Christ's divinity.

## Relationship to doctrine

(C) Novatian reads John 17:5 as Christ speaking of glory he had with the Father before the world existed, proving his divinity. (B) The step from 'glory before the world' to 'therefore God' is Novatian's inference, labelled as such. (D) The reading predates Nicaea; Novatian's Son is eternal in glory yet derived from the Father.

## Historical context

Treatise Concerning the Trinity, c. 250 (approximate), ch. 13, in the cumulative scriptural proof that Christ is God.
