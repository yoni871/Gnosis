# Passages with no attested interpretation (Phase 3)

**Status: research notes, verification-pending. Not publishable without independent verification and editorial approval.**

Phase 3 found no attested historical interpretation for **23 of the 60** passages in `passages.json`. For 22 of them, at least one era researcher checked the works examined in that era and found no attested reading; for 1 (Jude 20) no era individually searched for a reading. "No attested interpretation found" means only that: no reading was found in the works actually examined. It is **not** a claim that no historical writer anywhere interpreted the passage, and it is not proof of absence. The per-era scope statements below say exactly what was examined.

## Scope of what was examined (per era)

- **Era 1 (c. 90–200):** Justin Martyr, *Dialogue* chs. 55–68 and *First Apology* chs. 6, 61, 65, 67; Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* III.6, III.11, III.17, IV.20; 1 Clement ch. 46; Theophilus, *To Autolycus* II.15, II.18, II.22; Athenagoras, *Plea* ch. 10; Ignatius, *Ephesians* chs. 7, 9; *Martyrdom of Polycarp* ch. 14. Not examined: other *Dialogue* chapters, Tatian, Melito, Barnabas, Didache, Hermas.
- **Era 2 (c. 200–400):** Tertullian, *Against Praxeas* chs. 2, 12; Novatian, *Treatise Concerning the Trinity* ch. 13; Cyprian, *On the Unity of the Church* §§4–6; Origen, *De Principiis* I.2; Athanasius, *Against the Arians* Discourse I §§1–7, Discourse II §1, Discourse IV §§1–7; Basil, *On the Holy Spirit* ch. 16 §§37–40, ch. 25 §59; Gregory of Nazianzus, Oration 31 §§1–9, 26; Hilary, *On the Trinity* I §§1–17. Not checked in this pass: Eusebius, Gregory of Nyssa, Ambrose, Cyril of Jerusalem, Didymus.
- **Era 3 (c. 400–800):** Augustine, *De Trinitate* (esp. Books II, XII) and *Contra Maximinum* II.22; Cyril of Alexandria, *Commentary on John*; John of Damascus, *Exposition* I.8; Leo I, *Letter 15*. No passage in this list was individually searched in Era 3 beyond the two noted in its none-found file (Gen 19:24, Deut 6:4 — both covered by interpretations from other eras).
- **Era 4 (c. 800–1500):** Aquinas, *Summa Theologiae* I, qq. 27, 30–34, 36–39, 41–43; III, q. 66; Anselm, *Monologion* ch. 33. Not fetched: *Summa* I, qq. 28, 29, 35, 40; other *Monologion* chapters; Abelard, Richard of St Victor, Bonaventure, Duns Scotus, Ockham, Photius, Palamas.
- **Era 5 (c. 1500–1900):** Calvin, *Institutes* I.xiii, *Commentary on John* chs. 1, 10, 14, *Commentaries on the Catholic Epistles* (1 John 5); Luther, *Commentary on Galatians* ch. 4; Racovian Catechism III.1, IV.1; Edwards, *Essay on the Trinity*; Augsburg Confession Art. I; Westminster Confession ch. 2. Of the passages below, none was individually searched in Era 5 except as listed in its none-found file; Zwingli and Schleiermacher were not checked at all.

## Notes
- Romans 8:14 and 1 Corinthians 8:6 were cited in passing by Basil (*On the Holy Spirit*) but not as developed interpretations, so no records were forced; 1 Cor 8:6 is covered by interpretations from Era 1 (Irenaeus) and Era 5 (Racovian Catechism), while Rom 8:14 remains uncovered.
- John 14:26 was checked in Era 2 (Gregory, Oration 31 §26 discusses the Spirit's sending) and Era 4 (Aquinas cites John 14:16 and 15:26 in the questions fetched, not 14:26); it is covered by an Era 3 interpretation (Augustine, *De Trinitate*).
- 1 John 5:7 (Johannine Comma) is covered by interpretations (Cyprian, Augustine, Aquinas, Calvin, Lateran IV, Westminster, Racovian Catechism); the Comma's originality is never asserted — see the Johannine Comma note in the README.
