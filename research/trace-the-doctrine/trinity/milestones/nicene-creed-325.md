# nicene-creed-325

- Passages: `john-1-1`
- Authors: none
- Status: verification-pending

## Summary

(D) The Nicene Creed of 325 confesses 'one Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the only-begotten of his Father, of the substance of the Father, God of God, Light of Light, very God of very God, begotten, not made, being of one substance with the Father. By whom all things were made.' It ends with the brief clause 'And [we believe] in the Holy Ghost', followed by anathemas against those who say there was a time when the Son was not. The creed's Son language turns on John 1:1 ('the Word was God', 'all things were made by him'); (A) the verse itself does not state the creed's terms.
