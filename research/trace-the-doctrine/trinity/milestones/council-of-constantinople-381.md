- Status: verification-pending

## Summary

(D) The Council of Constantinople (381) reaffirmed the Nicene faith and is associated with the received form of the creed that expands the confession of the Holy Spirit: 'the Lord and Giver-of-Life, who proceedeth from the Father, who with the Father and the Son together is worshipped and glorified, who spake by the prophets.' The Spirit clause turns on John 15:26 ('the Spirit of truth which proceedeth from the Father'), the text Gregory of Nazianzus had expounded in his Fifth Theological Oration (380). (A) John 15:26 itself states that the Spirit proceeds from the Father; it does not state the creed's worship clause. Gregory of Nazianzus led the council during part of its sessions; he is not the author of the creed.
