# council-of-nicaea-325

- Title: Council of Nicaea

- Date: 325 (exact)
- Passages: `john-1-1`
- Authors: `athanasius`, `arius`
- Status: verification-pending

## Summary

(D) The Council of Nicaea (325) condemned Arius and set forth a creed confessing the Son as 'the only-begotten of his Father, of the substance of the Father, God of God, Light of Light, very God of very God, begotten, not made, being of one substance with the Father', and anathematised all who say 'there was a time when the Son of God was not', or that he was made of things that were not, or is a creature. The council's use of 'of one substance' (homoousios) turned on John 1:1 and the question whether the Word 'was God' eternally or was made. (A) John 1:1 itself does not use the word homoousios; the council's term is a later formulation (D) applied to the text, not a statement of the text.

## Notes

Athanasius, present at Nicaea as a deacon, is listed as an author involved; he did not author the creed. Arius is listed as the writer whose teaching was condemned. The Nicene Creed is also recorded as a separate creed milestone (nicene-creed-325).
