# 1 Peter 1:10-12

- **Reference:** 1 Peter 1:10-12 (canonical position 60)
- **Classification:** indirect
- **Justification:** The passage states the Spirit's identification with Christ in the prophetic ministry ("the Spirit of Christ in them"), but its statement is about prophetic ministry, and the trinitarian weight is an inference.
- **Relationship (A–D):** (A) The text states the prophets inquired what person or time "the Spirit of Christ in them was indicating when he predicted the sufferings of Christ and the subsequent glories", and the good news was announced "by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven". (B) Inference, labelled as inference: the identification of the Spirit in the prophets with Christ is consistent with and supports the later doctrine that the Spirit is the Spirit of the Son and shares the divine life. (C) How later writers read the passage is a matter for Phase 3; no historical reading is asserted here. (D) The later formulation of the Trinity is dated generally as later and is never projected back onto the passage as its explicit teaching.
- **Interpretive questions and alternatives:**
  - Is "the Spirit of Christ" the Holy Spirit identified with Christ, or Christ's own spirit present in the prophets?
  - Does verse 12's "the Holy Spirit sent from heaven" identify the same Spirit as "the Spirit of Christ" in verse 11?
  - Alternative: the phrase may describe the Spirit as belonging to Christ or sent by Christ without asserting identity of person.
