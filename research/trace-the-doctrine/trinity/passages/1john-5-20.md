# 1 John 5:20

- **Reference:** 1 John 5:20 (canonical position 62)
- **Classification:** indirect
- **Justification:** The verse says "He is the true God and eternal life", but the antecedent of "He"/"This" (Jesus Christ or God the Father) is disputed. The ambiguity is recorded, not resolved silently, so the verse cannot be classed as a direct ascription of "God" to the Son.
- **Relationship (A–D):** (A) The text states: "we are in him who is true, in his Son Jesus Christ. He is the true God and eternal life". The Son of God has come and given understanding so believers may know him who is true. (B) Inference, labelled as inference: if "He" refers to Jesus Christ, the verse ascribes "the true God" to the Son, which supports the doctrine by implication; but the antecedent is disputed and the ambiguity is recorded, not resolved silently. (C) How later writers read the passage is a matter for Phase 3; no historical reading is asserted here. (D) The later formulation of the Trinity is dated generally as later and is never projected back onto the verse as its explicit teaching.
- **Interpretive questions and alternatives:**
  - Does "This"/"He" ("He is the true God") refer to Jesus Christ, the nearest antecedent, or to God the Father ("him who is true")?
  - Alternative: if "This" refers to the Father, the verse does not ascribe "true God" to the Son at all.
  - How does the parallel in John 17:3, where "the only true God" is the Father, bear on the reading of 1 John 5:20?
