# 1 John 5:7-8

- **Reference:** 1 John 5:7-8 (canonical position 62)
- **Classification:** indirect — TEXTUAL-VARIANT FLAG (most important flag in the whole inventory)
- **Justification:** The authentic text speaks of three witnesses — the Spirit, the water, and the blood — and does not name Father, Son, and Holy Spirit together. The famous trinitarian wording (the Johannine Comma) is a later addition and must not be presented as direct evidence.
- **Relationship (A–D):** (A) The text states (ESV, checked via BibleGateway): "For there are three that testify: the Spirit and the water and the blood; and these three agree." The KJV text of the same verses, also checked, includes the Comma: "For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one." (B) Inference, labelled as inference: the three witnesses are consistent with the testimony concerning the Son, and the passage supports the doctrine only by implication through that testimony, not by naming the three persons. (C) How later writers read the passage is a matter for Phase 3; no historical reading is asserted here. (D) The later formulation of the Trinity is dated generally as later and is never projected back onto the passage; the Comma must not be presented as direct evidence, since it is a later textual addition, not part of the original text.
- **Interpretive questions and alternatives:**
  - Which reading is original: the three witnesses (the Spirit, the water, and the blood) or the Johannine Comma naming the Father, the Word, and the Holy Spirit?
  - What do "the water" and "the blood" refer to, and how do they testify concerning the Son?
  - Alternative: the Comma, if treated as original, would make the passage direct evidence; the evidence checked here (ESV omitting it, KJV including it) shows the editions differ, so on what basis is the Comma excluded from direct evidence?
- **Cross-references:** matt-28-19, 2cor-13-14, john-15-26
- **Textual-variant / uncertainty flags:** TEXTUAL-VARIANT FLAG: The Johannine Comma ("the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one") is a later textual addition, absent from the ESV text checked and present in the KJV text checked. It must not be presented as direct evidence. The Comma's absence from the earliest Greek manuscripts and presence in later Latin tradition is the standard scholarly position as reported here, not something verified manuscript by manuscript in this check. No manuscripts were examined first-hand.
